# Register and Provision

Make it easy for conference attendees to join, and make sure everyone arrives ready to build.

## Do this

1. **Offer a hackathon ticket or add-on,** with a student option and a clear note on whether it includes the conference.
2. **Collect what sponsors need** at registration: name, email, organization, role, and city.
3. **Provision accounts and credits** from the registration list before the day.
4. **Send the hackathon guide** a week ahead, with where to go inside the conference venue.
5. **Promote it through the conference:** the website, emails, social posts, and the program.
