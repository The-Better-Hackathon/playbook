# Plan Around the Schedule

Decide how the hackathon fits with the conference days.

## Do this

1. **Choose when it runs.**

    | Option | Good for | Example |
    |---|---|---|
    | **The day before** | A full 12-hour hack day, then demos during the conference | Michigan DevFest + AI Hackathon (Friday before sessions) |
    | **During the conference** | Keeping hackers connected to talks and sponsors | AfroHacks at AfroTech |
    | **Across two days** | A 24-hour hackathon that ends as the conference opens | |

2. **Pick a format** that fits: usually a [hack day](../library/formats/hack-day.md) or [24 hours](../library/formats/24-hour.md).
3. **Avoid clashes** with keynotes and the sessions your participants most want to see.
4. **Book the demo slot** on the main stage before you set the submission deadline.

!!! success "Ready for the next step when"
    The hackathon schedule and the demo slot are in the conference program.
