# Partner With the Conference

Agree on the basics with the conference organizers before anything is announced.

## Do this

1. **Agree on the goal:** what the hackathon adds to the conference, and who it's for.
2. **Settle registration:** a separate registration you control, or an add-on to the conference ticket. Either way, get full access to the participant list. See [Registration and attendee data](../library/planning.md#registration-and-attendee-data).
3. **Confirm space and time:** a room with power and Wi-Fi, and whether it overlaps with conference sessions.
4. **Ask for main stage time** for the winners, and a spot in the conference program and emails.
5. **Agree on branding:** the hackathon keeps its own name and sponsors, and the conference is credited.
