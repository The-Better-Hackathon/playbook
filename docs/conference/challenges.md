# Tie Challenges to the Conference

The best conference hackathons feel like part of the conference, not a side event.

## Do this

1. **Match the conference themes.** An AI conference gets AI challenges; a community conference gets local impact challenges.
2. **Invite conference sponsors** to bring a challenge, tools and credits, or a workshop. Share the [Partner track](../partner/index.md).
3. **Write challenge briefs** with the solution left to the teams. See [Co-created AI challenges](../challenges/index.md).
4. **Offer more than one way to take part** if it fits, such as open source contribution or a design sprint. See [Track options](../library/tracks.md).

!!! success "Ready for the next step when"
    Challenges and tracks are published alongside the conference program.
