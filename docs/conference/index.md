A conference hackathon runs alongside a conference: the day before, during, or as a track. It brings builders into the conference, and gives the conference something to show on its main stage.

**Examples:** the **Michigan DevFest + AI Hackathon**, a 12-hour hack day on the Friday before the conference sessions, and **AfroHacks at AfroTech**, the first hackathon at the AfroTech Conference.

## Why run one

- **For the conference:** hands-on energy, a reason for builders to attend, and demos for the main stage.
- **For sponsors:** a way to see their tools in action, beyond a booth.
- **For participants:** a hackathon where the audience, speakers, and employers are already in the building.
