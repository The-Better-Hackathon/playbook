# Demo on the Main Stage

The main stage is what makes a conference hackathon special: builders get the biggest audience of the event.

## Do this

1. **Give finalists short, fixed demo slots,** such as three minutes each.
2. **Announce winners on stage,** with sponsors presenting their own prizes.
3. **Recognize every team** with a slide or a shout-out, not only the finalists.
4. **Share results** in the conference recap and on social.
5. **Follow up** with participants, sponsors, and the conference organizers.
