# Report Outcomes

Regional partners renew when they can see what the event produced for them and for the region.

## Do this

1. **Send each challenge partner a report** within two weeks: the projects built for their challenge, the teams, and next steps.
2. **Track regional outcomes:** pilots with local companies, hires and internships in the region, startups formed, and projects still going at 30 and 90 days.
3. **Share a public recap** with your economic development partners and the community.
4. **Set next year's date** and invite partners back while the results are fresh.
