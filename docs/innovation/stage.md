# Winners Take the Stage

In the Innovation Model, winning comes with a bigger audience: the people who can fund, hire, pilot, and spread the work.

## Do this

1. **Put winners on your region's biggest stage** within days of the event, like a tech week or conference.
2. **Help them prepare:** a short talk, a demo, and one slide with what they need next.
3. **Invite the challenge partners and economic development leaders** to watch.
4. **Publish every winning write-up** with the presentation and demo, so they stay public.
