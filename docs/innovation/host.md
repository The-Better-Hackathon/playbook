# Host the Event

The shape of Hack Michigan's three days. Adjust for your format.

| Day | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Day 1** | Check-in, welcome with the challenge partners presenting their own challenges, an AI tools and provisioning workshop, a Design for AI workshop, networking, and hacking begins |
| **Day 2** | Morning mindfulness, How to Win a Hackathon, hands-on workshops with partner tools, office hours, dinner, and hacking continues |
| **Day 3** | Code freeze, finish presentations, submissions due, practice demos, judging, and awards |

## Do this

1. **Let partners present their own challenges** at the welcome.
2. **Coach every team** with the [How to Win workshop and team workbook](../win/index.md).
3. **Keep partners in the room** for office hours, so teams can ask about the real problem.
4. **Judge against each challenge's criteria and test,** plus how the work keeps value in the region.
5. **Care for people across three days:** meals, rest, and wellbeing. See [The participant experience](../run/experience.md).
