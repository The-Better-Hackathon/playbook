# Co-Create Industry Challenges

This is the heart of the model. Each partner brings a real problem; you turn it into a brief teams can solve.

## Do this

1. **Hold an intro call with each partner:** their problem, the people who have it, what data they can share, and what success would look like.
2. **Take out the solutioning.** Partners often arrive with a solution in mind. Keep the problem and the outcomes; leave the build to the teams.
3. **Write the brief** in seven sections, ending with one test judges will apply to every demo.
4. **Add a regional challenge** for teams that want to build for the region as a whole, like the Michigan Innovation Renaissance challenge.
5. **Review each brief with its partner,** then publish them all together.
