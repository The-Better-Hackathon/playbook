# Rally Your Region's Partners

The Innovation Model is a regional effort. Bring together the organizations that care about keeping talent and opportunity local.

## Do this

1. **Start with a small organizing team** from your region's tech community: a nonprofit, a developer group, a university, or a coalition.
2. **Recruit challenge partners** from your region's anchor industries (manufacturing, energy, mobility, health, agriculture, small business) and from startup support organizations. Share the [Partner track](../partner/index.md).
3. **Recruit technology partners** for AI tools, cloud credits, and workshops.
4. **Bring in economic development partners:** your city or state economic development agency, chambers of commerce, and foundations.
5. **Find your bigger stage:** a tech week, a regional conference, or a partner's event where winners will present.
