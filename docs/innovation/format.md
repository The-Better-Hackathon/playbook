# Choose Your Format

Hack Michigan runs 48 hours over three days, which gives teams time to work with real data. Smaller regions or first events can start shorter.

## Do this

1. **Pick a format.**

    | Format | When to choose it |
    |---|---|
    | [48 hours](../run/formats/48-hour.md) | The full model: deep, data-heavy industry challenges |
    | [24 hours](../run/formats/24-hour.md) | A first year, or a university-hosted event |
    | [Hack day](../run/formats/hack-day.md) | Alongside a conference, with one or two tightly scoped challenges |

2. **Pick a venue** at the center of your region's innovation community, like an innovation hub, incubator, or campus.
3. **Time it with your stage.** Hack Michigan ran the weekend before Michigan Tech Week, so winners presented days later.
4. **Add tracks** if they fit your partners, such as local impact or sustainability. See [Track options](../run/tracks.md).

!!! success "Ready for the next step when"
    You have a format, a venue, and dates that line up with your stage.
