## How teams use it

1. Open the workbook link and choose **File → Make a copy**.
2. Name it with your team name and share it with your teammates.
3. Fill in every slide marked **✏️ Workbook**.

Prefer the web? Use the [interactive workbook](workbook.md).

## Running the workshop

- Slides marked ✏️ are questions; answer one or two together in the room and leave the rest as team time.
- The Event Info slide makes the same deck work for any event.
- The speaker notes give clock times for a 60-minute session.
