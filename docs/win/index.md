A 60-minute workshop and a team workbook. The workshop teaches the eight steps; the workbook is where your team answers the questions for each one.

## The eight steps

| Step | What you do | Workbook questions |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Come up with an idea | Start with the challenge, a real person, and a real problem, not the tech. | Challenge and its test, the problem, user, why it matters, why you care, problem statement |
| 2. Research the idea | Size the problem and see what already exists. | Impact data, existing solutions, where they fail, do it better, design for AI |
| 3. Finalize your idea | Make sure it is valuable, realistic, and new. | Business value, impact, realistic, innovation, market, competitors, monetization |
| 4. Determine the MVP | Build on what exists. Keep it simple. | AI frameworks, open-source tools, scope |
| 5. Hack it out | Split the work, plan the time, know the rules. | Team roles, build plan, tech and data rules |
| 6. Build a pitch deck | Tell a story with your user as the hero. | Be passionate, tell a story |
| 7. Practice the demo | Test it, record it, practice it with another team. | 60-second video script, feedback, questions |
| 8. Submit on time | Design toward the rubric and the challenge's test, and submit early. | Judging criteria map, what happens next, submission checklist |

## Two ways to use the workbook

- **In the room:** the Google Slides workbook. One copy per team; fill in every slide marked ✏️ Workbook. See [Workshop decks](decks.md).
- **On the web:** the [interactive workbook](workbook.md). Same questions, saved in your browser, exported as Markdown.

## Sizing the workshop to the event

=== "Hack day"

    **30 minutes, first thing.** Focus on the idea, the scope, roles and plan, the demo video, and the checklist.

=== "24 hours"

    **45 to 60 minutes, once teams have an idea.** All eight steps; market and competition are team time.

=== "48 hours"

    **60 minutes, the morning of day two.** All eight steps.

See [Using the workbook in each format](../run/formats/index.md#using-the-workbook-in-each-format).

## KISS

Skip the login. Focus on the main function. Build the main view. Add at most one extra feature. Document future features. And ask: are you solving the problem, or a symptom?
