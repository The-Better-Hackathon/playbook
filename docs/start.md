# Start Here

Pick what describes you. Each path is a few short steps, in order. You don't need to read the whole playbook.

=== "I'm participating"

    1. **Make your team's copy of the workbook.** [Open the team workbook](win/workbook.md)
    2. **Find your challenge** and write down its test. [See example challenges](challenges/index.md)
    3. **Learn the eight steps** from idea to demo. [How to Win a Hackathon](win/index.md)
    4. **Check the submission list** on day one, not the last hour. [Submit and demo](win/submit.md)

    Rather contribute to open source than build from scratch? See [Ways to participate](run/participation.md).

=== "I'm organizing"

    Follow the **[Organize track](organize/index.md)**: seven short steps, one page each.

    1. [Choose your format](organize/format.md)
    2. [Decide how people take part](organize/participation.md)
    3. [Bring in partners](organize/partners.md)
    4. [Design the experience](organize/experience.md)
    5. [Prepare](organize/prepare.md)
    6. [Host the day](organize/host.md)
    7. [Follow up and celebrate](organize/follow-up.md)

=== "I'm a sponsor or partner"

    Follow the **[Partner track](partner/index.md)**: five short steps, one page each.

    1. [Choose how you'll take part](partner/ways-to-partner.md)
    2. [Shape your challenge](partner/shape-your-challenge.md)
    3. [Show up for the teams](partner/event-day.md)
    4. [Follow up](partner/follow-up.md)
    5. [Measure outcomes](partner/outcomes.md)

=== "I'm running an AfroHacks"

    Follow the **[AfroHacks guide](afrohacks/index.md)**: six short steps for communities, universities, and HBCUs.

=== "I'm running an open source hackathon"

    Follow the **[Open source hackathon guide](open-source/index.md)**: six short steps to build with, contribute to, and improve open source.

=== "I'm with a university"

    1. **Pick a format** that fits your calendar. [Choose your format](organize/format.md)
    2. **Bring in industry and community partners** to co-create challenges. [Bring in partners](organize/partners.md)
    3. **Use the workshop and workbook** with students. [Workshop decks](win/decks.md)
    4. **Plan outcomes** students can put in a portfolio. [Outcomes](partner/outcomes.md)

=== "I'm running a community meetup"

    1. **Start with a pop-up hack day** in a public space. [Pop-up hack day](run/formats/pop-up.md)
    2. **Make everyone feel welcome.** [The participant experience](run/experience.md)
    3. **Offer an open source path** for people who want to contribute. [Ways to participate](run/participation.md)
