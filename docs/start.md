# Start Here

Pick what describes you. Each path is a few short steps. You don't need to read the whole playbook.

=== "I'm participating"

    1. **Make your team's copy of the workbook.** [Open the team workbook](participate/workbook.md)
    2. **Find your challenge** and write down its test. [See example challenges](challenges/index.md)
    3. **Learn the eight steps** from idea to demo. [How to Win a Hackathon](participate/index.md)
    4. **Check the submission list** on day one, not the last hour. [Submit and demo](participate/submit.md)

=== "I'm organizing"

    **Start from a model** if one fits:

    - [Hack Your Region](innovation/index.md): a regional hackathon with industry partners, based on Hack Michigan
    - [AfroHacks](afrohacks/index.md): for communities, universities, and HBCUs
    - [Open source hackathon](open-source/index.md): build with, contribute to, and improve open source
    - [Conference hackathon](conference/index.md): alongside a conference, with demos on the main stage
    - [Community pop-up](pop-up/index.md): 4 to 8 hours in a public space

    **Or [Design Your Own](organize/index.md)** in seven short steps.

    Want a plan made for your event? **[Build Your Playbook](organize/build-your-playbook.md)** with the builder, an AI prompt, or the Skill.

=== "I'm a sponsor or partner"

    Follow the **[Partner track](partner/index.md)**: five short steps, one page each.

    1. [Choose how you'll take part](partner/ways-to-partner.md)
    2. [Shape your challenge](partner/shape-your-challenge.md)
    3. [Show up for the teams](partner/event-day.md)
    4. [Follow up](partner/follow-up.md)
    5. [Measure outcomes](partner/outcomes.md)
