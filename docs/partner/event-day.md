# Show Up for the Teams

Your presence makes your challenge real. Teams build better work when they can meet the people behind the problem.

## Do this

1. **Present your challenge** at the opening in five minutes or less: the problem, who has it, and the test you'll judge by.
2. **Run your workshop,** if you have one. Keep it hands-on and share your slides.
3. **Walk the room.** Visit teams working on your challenge, answer questions about the problem, and point them to data.
4. **Judge with curiosity.** Use the published criteria and the challenge's test. Thank every team, and give feedback beyond the finalists.
5. **Be welcoming.** Every interaction is part of the participant experience.

## Please don't

- Pitch your products or hiring in place of helping teams
- Hint at the solution you'd build yourself
- Judge on criteria that weren't published
