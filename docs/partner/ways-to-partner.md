# Choose How You'll Take Part

There's more than one way to partner. Pick one or combine several.

| Way to take part | What you give | What you get | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Bring a challenge** | A real problem, and context or data | Working prototypes built for your problem, and the people who built them | A few hours before, plus the event |
| **Provide tools and credits** | Platform access or cloud credits | Your tools used on real problems, and builder feedback | Setup before the event |
| **Run a workshop** | 30 to 60 minutes teaching your tools or expertise | Visibility, and builders who know your platform | Half a day |
| **Mentor and judge** | People who visit teams and judge | Direct contact with builders and their work | Part of the event |
| **Offer a prize or next step** | Cash, swag, or a pilot conversation, stage, or feature | Winners who want to keep working with you | Minimal |
| **Contribute a starter kit** | Sample data, an API quickstart, or a template repo | Stronger work on your challenge, less time lost to setup | A few hours before |
| **Host or feed the event** | A venue, meals, or coffee | Your space and brand at the center of the event | Varies |
| **Bring open source projects** | Maintainers and starter issues | New contributors to your projects | A few hours before, plus the event |

The most valuable combination is a challenge plus a person for the day. A next-step prize, like a pilot conversation, is worth more to most teams than cash.
