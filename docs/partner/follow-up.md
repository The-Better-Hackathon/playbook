# Follow Up

The best outcomes happen after the event: a pilot, a hire, a project that keeps going.

## Do this

1. **Review the write-ups and demos** for your challenge within a week.
2. **Meet the teams** whose work you want to take further. The organizer can make introductions.
3. **Offer a next step:** a pilot conversation, a user test, more data, an internship, or a stage to present.
4. **Celebrate the winners** on your own channels.
5. **Tell the organizer what happened,** so it can go in the event's outcomes.

??? question "Who owns what the teams build?"
    Set this in the event's official rules before registration opens. Participants commonly keep ownership of their work; if you want a path to use it, plan for a follow-up agreement with the team. Check with your legal team.
