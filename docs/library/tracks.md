# Track Options

A track is a way to take part, with its own goal, judging, and prizes. Most events run one to three. Pick the tracks that fit your community and partners, and keep the rest of the event the same.

## At a glance

| Track | What teams do | Best for | Format |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Challenge build** | Build a prototype for a real challenge | Industry and community partners | Any |
| **Open source contribution** | Contribute to existing projects | Foundations, projects, newcomers | Any |
| **Capture the flag** | Solve security puzzles to find hidden "flags" | Security learners and pros | Hack day or longer |
| **Bug bash** | Find, report, and fix bugs in a product or project | Product teams, open source projects | Hack day |
| **Design sprint** | Research, design, and prototype an experience | Designers, researchers, product people | Any |
| **Accessibility** | Audit and fix accessibility issues | Everyone; pairs well with design | Any |
| **Docs sprint** | Write and improve documentation and tutorials | Projects with docs gaps, writers, newcomers | Pop-up or hack day |
| **AI evaluation and data** | Build evaluations, datasets, and model cards | Data scientists, researchers | Hack day or longer |
| **Local impact** | Build for a need in your city or campus | Community partners | Any |
| **Beginner** | Guided first project with mentors | First-timers and students | Any |
| **Hardware and makers** | Build with devices, sensors, and boards | Makers, engineering students | 24 hours or longer |
| **Sustainability** | Build for climate, energy, and efficiency | Utilities, cities, foundations | Any |

## Track details

??? abstract "Challenge build"
    The core of the model: teams build a working prototype for a partner's real challenge.

    - **Prepare:** challenge briefs, tools and credits, a starter kit
    - **Judge on:** the challenge's own criteria and its test
    - **See:** [Co-created AI challenges](../challenges/index.md)

??? abstract "Open source contribution"
    Participants make real contributions to existing projects.

    - **Prepare:** projects, maintainers, starter issues, a setup session
    - **Judge on:** impact, quality, following the project's process, collaboration
    - **See:** [Run an Open Source Hackathon](../open-source/index.md)

??? abstract "Capture the flag (CTF)"
    Teams solve security challenges (web, cryptography, forensics, reverse engineering) to find hidden text strings called flags. Each flag earns points.

    - **Prepare:** a scoring platform (CTFd is a popular open source option), challenges at several difficulty levels, and an isolated environment built for the event
    - **Rules of engagement:** attack only the challenge environment. Never the venue network, other teams, or any real system. Publish this in the rules and repeat it at the opening.
    - **Judge on:** points on the scoreboard, with a write-up prize for the clearest explanation of a solve
    - **Tip:** include easy warm-up challenges so beginners score early

??? abstract "Bug bash"
    Teams find, report, and fix bugs in a partner's product or an open source project.

    - **Prepare:** a test build or staging environment, a bug report template, and a triage team from the partner
    - **Scoring:** points by severity (for example critical, high, medium, low), extra points for a fix, and none for duplicates
    - **Rules:** test only the environment provided. Report security issues privately to the partner, never in public.
    - **Judge on:** severity, reproducibility, report quality, and fixes

??? abstract "Design sprint"
    Teams research, design, and prototype an experience, without needing to write production code.

    - **Prepare:** a design challenge, access to real users or research, and design tools
    - **Deliverables:** research findings, user flows, and a clickable prototype
    - **Judge on:** understanding of the user, usability, accessibility, and storytelling
    - **See:** Design for AI in the [How to Win workshop](../participate/index.md)

??? abstract "Accessibility"
    Teams audit a site, app, or open source project for accessibility and fix what they find.

    - **Prepare:** target sites or projects, accessibility checklists and tools, and ideally testers who use assistive technology
    - **Judge on:** severity of issues found and fixed, and quality of the fixes

??? abstract "Docs sprint"
    Contributors write tutorials, fix outdated guides, and improve onboarding for a project or product.

    - **Prepare:** a list of documentation gaps from maintainers, and style guides
    - **Judge on:** accuracy, clarity, and how much faster a newcomer can get started

??? abstract "AI evaluation and data"
    Teams build evaluations, benchmarks, datasets, and model or dataset cards for open models.

    - **Prepare:** open models and datasets, compute or credits, and evaluation frameworks
    - **Judge on:** rigor, reproducibility, documentation, and what the work reveals about bias or quality

??? abstract "Local impact"
    Teams build for a need in their own city or campus, with the people who have it.

    - **Prepare:** community partners who describe real needs, and a rule to talk to someone who has the problem
    - **Example:** the A2Tech360 Local Impact track, for Ann Arbor and Washtenaw County

??? abstract "Beginner"
    A guided track for first-time hackers, with mentors and a simpler goal.

    - **Prepare:** a starter template, dedicated mentors, and a short build guide
    - **Judge on:** learning and completion, with prizes only beginners can win

??? abstract "Hardware and makers"
    Teams build with microcontrollers, sensors, and single-board computers. Hardware needs more time than software: plan about a third of build time for assembly and fitting parts together.

    - **Prepare:** a hardware library to borrow from, soldering and safety rules, and a maker space or lab
    - **Run the library:** label every part, log who borrows what, and hold an ID until it comes back. Staff it in shifts, near the help desk
    - **Start with basics:** microcontroller boards, single-board computers, sensor kits, breadboards, and wires. Test every kit before handing it out, and pre-solder headers
    - **Set up zones:** assembly, testing, and fabrication (3D printers, laser cutters), with trained supervisors at hot tools
    - **Plan demos:** projects that move on a tray or cart, a short reset checklist, and backup video in case something breaks
    - **Ask participants** to install drivers and software before they arrive
    - **Judge on:** a working device, creativity, and usefulness

??? abstract "Sustainability"
    Teams build for climate, energy, efficiency, and resilience.

    - **Prepare:** partners from utilities, cities, or foundations, and public environmental data
    - **Example:** the DTE Energy [Utility Pole Risk Profiling](../challenges/examples/dte-energy.md) challenge

## Tips for running more than one track

- **Workshops are open to all,** whatever track people choose.
- **Let people pick at registration,** and switch on the day if they need to.
- **Give each track its own judges and prizes,** and celebrate every track at the awards.
- **Say whether a project can win in more than one track.**
