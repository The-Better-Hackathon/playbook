# 48 Hours (3 Days)

A 48-hour hackathon over three days. This is the Hack Michigan format: the depth sponsor challenges need, with time for workshops, mentoring, and rest. The schedule below is Hack Michigan 2026 (May 15–17, TechTown Detroit). Swap in your own workshops and times; keep the shape.

| Time | Block | Why it's here |
|---|---|---|
| 2:00 PM | Check-in and registration | |

| 6:00 PM | Happy hour and networking | Team formation happens here |

## What's different about 48 hours

- **Sponsors present their own challenges** at the welcome. It sets the bar and gives teams a face to build for.
- **Hacking starts in the evening,** after the tools and Design for AI workshops and a happy hour where teams form.
- **Day two is for learning while building:** How to Win in the morning, then hands-on sponsor workshops and office hours.
- **Wellbeing is part of the design.** Morning mindfulness and yoga, real meals, and a movie night keep people going.
- **Protect the finish.** Code freeze two hours before the deadline, an hour to finish presentations, and practice demos before judging.
- **Winners go to a bigger stage** within days. See [After the event](../after.md).
