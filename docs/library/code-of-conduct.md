# Code of Conduct

*COMPASS: Collective of Minority Professionals and STEAM Societies*

## Purpose

COMPASS is dedicated to providing a welcoming, inclusive, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of gender, gender identity and expression, sexual orientation, disability, physical appearance, body size, race, age, religion, or technology choices. We do not tolerate harassment of participants in any form. This Code of Conduct outlines our expectations for participant behavior as well as the consequences for unacceptable behavior.

## Expected behavior

1. **Respect and integrity**
    - Treat everyone with respect and consideration, valuing a diversity of views and opinions.
    - Communicate openly and professionally, avoiding personal attacks and offensive comments.
2. **Inclusivity**
    - Welcome individuals from all backgrounds and make an effort to include everyone in discussions and activities.
    - Actively seek diverse perspectives and contributions, ensuring all voices are heard and respected.
3. **Professionalism**
    - Be considerate and respectful in all interactions, whether in person or online.
    - Refrain from demeaning, discriminatory, or harassing behavior and speech.
4. **Support and collaboration**
    - Foster a supportive environment where collaboration and mentorship are encouraged.
    - Offer help and guidance to those who seek it, promoting a culture of continuous learning and growth.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, bullying, or discrimination in any form.
- Physical or verbal abuse of any participant.
- Intimidation, stalking, or following.
- Inappropriate physical contact or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Disrespectful or disruptive behavior during events, meetings, or discussions.
- Making offensive comments related to gender, sexual orientation, disability, physical appearance, race, or religion.
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or electronic address, without explicit permission.

## Reporting incidents

If you are subjected to unacceptable behavior, notice that someone else is being subjected to unacceptable behavior, or have any other concerns, please notify a COMPASS representative as soon as possible. Reports can be made anonymously or personally. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.

## Consequences of unacceptable behavior

Unacceptable behavior from any participant, including sponsors and those with decision-making authority, will not be tolerated. Anyone asked to stop unacceptable behavior is expected to comply immediately. If a participant engages in unacceptable behavior, COMPASS may take any action they deem appropriate, including expulsion from the event or community without warning or refund.

## Acknowledgement

By participating in COMPASS, you agree to abide by this Code of Conduct and cooperate with the organizers to ensure a safe and inclusive environment for everyone.
