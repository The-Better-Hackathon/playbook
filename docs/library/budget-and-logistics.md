# Budget and Logistics

Food is usually the biggest cost, and the venue is the biggest risk. Settle both early, then plan everything else around how many people will actually show up.

## Plan for who actually shows up

Free events lose about half their registrants. Plan food, swag, and space for the people you expect, not the registration count. See [registration numbers](planning.md#registration-numbers) for targets.

| Meal (24-hour event) | Share of registrants to plan for |
|---|---|
| Day 1 lunch | About 90% |
| Day 1 dinner | About 80% |
| Midnight snack | About 70% |
| Day 2 breakfast | About 30% |
| Day 2 lunch | About 60% |

Order generously early and taper off. Line up a shelter, food bank, or campus pantry ahead of time to take what's left.

## Venue

Book early. A confirmed venue makes partners and participants take the event seriously.

- [ ] **Power:** about 2.5 outlets per person, with power strips at every table
- [ ] **Wi-Fi:** enough for about 4 devices per person, tested at full load
- [ ] **Overnight:** allowed by the venue and by fire code, with a separate rest area
- [ ] **Fees:** ask whether security, cleaning, A/V, or a required caterer are mandatory, and get a quote with a ceiling
- [ ] **Outside food:** allowed, with a place to receive deliveries
- [ ] **Insurance:** what the venue requires from organizers and partners. See [Safety and emergency plan](safety.md)
- [ ] **Access:** step-free entrances, accessible restrooms, a quiet room, and clear signs
- [ ] **Rooms:** a main room, workshop rooms, a partner area, food away from laptops, and locked storage
- [ ] **Setup day:** the venue reserved the day before

On campus, find one staff advocate who can book rooms for you. A community space, library, or partner office often costs nothing. See [Community pop-up](../pop-up/index.md) for public spaces.

## Budget

Rough US figures. Get local quotes.

| Item | Rough cost | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Meals | $8 to $10 per person per meal | Usually the largest line |
| Snacks and drinks | About $10 per person per day | Coffee matters more than you think |
| T-shirts | $5 to $8 each | Avoid white; many fabrics are see-through |
| Prizes | Varies | See [Judging and prizes](judging-and-prizes.md) |
| Charter bus | About $3,300 to $3,500 per weekend | Prices rise in spring |
| Venue, A/V, security | $0 to a lot | Hidden fees can double a rental |
| Contingency | $1,000 to $3,000 | Scale to your event |

In this model, partners often cover a line directly: a venue, a meal, prizes, or swag. See [Bring in partners](../organize/partners.md#package-the-ask).

## Food

1. **Order 2 to 4 weeks ahead.** Check first whether the venue requires its own caterer.
2. **Ask about dietary needs at registration,** and ask volunteers, mentors, and judges too.
3. **Plan for** vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, dairy-free, nut allergies, halal, and kosher. If the caterer can't cover a need, offer a gift card and say so ahead of time.
4. **Keep a separate table** for special meals, with a printed list to check off names.
5. **Label every dish** with its allergens.
6. **Set up more than one serving line,** and call people up a few tables at a time.
7. **Time deliveries:** boxed meals 30 to 60 minutes before serving; buffets need 60 to 90 minutes to set up.
8. **Agree on pickup** for chafing dishes and anything else the caterer takes back.

## Travel and getting there

- **Send a travel note** a week out: the address, parking, transit, accessible entrances, and who to call if you're running late.
- **Reimbursement, if you offer it:** set simple caps (for example, a lower cap for driving alone and a higher one for a full carpool) and require receipts with the date and price.
- **Buses:** about one 56-seat bus per 70 people who say yes, since some won't come. Choose insured, well-reviewed companies, and have buses arrive 30 minutes early.

## Swag and prizes

- **Order 2 to 3 months ahead.** Many items get backordered.
- **T-shirt sizes:** most orders skew to medium and large. Use your registration data if you collect sizes.
- **Hand out swag across the event,** not all at check-in. It keeps people coming to workshops.
- **Keep receipts** so you can return what you don't use.
