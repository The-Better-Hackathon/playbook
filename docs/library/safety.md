# Safety and Emergency Plan

Write the plan before you need it, and make sure every organizer and volunteer knows where it is. Most of this takes an afternoon, and some of it needs sign-off from your venue or school, so start a month or more ahead.

## Before the event

- [ ] **Insurance:** check whether your organization's or school's event insurance covers a hackathon, and what the venue requires.
- [ ] **Agreements:** know who can sign partner agreements for your organization, and allow time for legal review.
- [ ] **Money:** decide where funds are held and how you'll pay vendors. Community organizers without a nonprofit can use a fiscal sponsor, an existing 501(c)(3) that holds funds for you.
- [ ] **Waivers and photo consent:** decide whether participants sign a liability waiver and a media release. Put both in registration.
- [ ] **Minors:** set a minimum age. If anyone under 18 attends, get parent or guardian consent, and check with the venue and legal counsel before any overnight. Never leave minors without an approved adult.
- [ ] **Data:** use registration data only for the event. Ask for consent before sharing it with partners, and say so on the form.
- [ ] **Code of Conduct:** published, with a way to report and named people who respond. See the [Code of Conduct](code-of-conduct.md).
- [ ] **Campus or building security:** told about the event, the hours, and the overnight plan.

## What the emergency plan covers

Keep it to one or two pages. Share it with every organizer and volunteer, and keep printed copies at the help desk.

| Section | What to write down |
|---|---|
| **Contacts** | Emergency number, campus or building security, non-emergency police, the nearest urgent care and hospital, and every lead organizer with the hours they're on duty |
| **Fire or evacuation** | Exits, the meeting point outside, and who checks each room |
| **Medical** | Where the first aid kit is, who is trained, and when to call emergency services |
| **Power or Wi-Fi outage** | Who calls the venue, the backup hotspot, and how you'll tell everyone |
| **Severe weather** | Where to shelter, and when you would pause or end the event |
| **Theft or lost items** | Where people report it, and where lost items are kept |
| **Code of Conduct report** | Who responds, where to talk privately, and how to follow up |

## During the event

- **One lead organizer is awake and on call** at all times, including overnight.
- **Keep the plan at the help desk,** with a first aid kit and a charged phone.
- **Keep aisles clear,** and tape down any cords that cross a walkway.
- **Separate the rest area** from the build space, with lights low and phones on silent.
- **Moderate online spaces too.** The Code of Conduct applies in Discord and in direct messages.
- **When something goes wrong,** contain it, stay calm, and tell people what's happening and what to do next.
