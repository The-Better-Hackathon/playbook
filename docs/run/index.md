[Hack Michigan](https://www.hackmichigan.com/) is a three-day AI hackathon produced by Compass Detroit with GDG Detroit. The 2026 event ran May 15–17 at TechTown Detroit, and the winners took the stage at Michigan Tech Week days later. This section is the model behind it, written so other organizers can run it as a community pop-up, a one-day hack day, a 24-hour weekend, or a full 48 hours.

## Principles

1. **Co-create real challenges.** Sponsors bring real business problems; the organizer writes the briefs. See [Challenges](../challenges/index.md).
2. **Outcomes, not specifications.** Briefs say what success looks like, never how to build it.
3. **Coach every team.** A tools and provisioning session, plus How to Win, give first-timers the same start as veterans.
4. **Protect the finish.** Code freeze before the submission deadline, then time to finish presentations and practice demos.
5. **Judge against published criteria.** Each challenge publishes its criteria up front, so teams design toward them.
6. **Give winners a bigger stage.** Winners present to the wider tech and business community, and their write-ups stay public.
7. **Keep it welcoming.** A Code of Conduct, mindfulness mornings, meals, and networking are part of the design, not extras.

## Timeline

| When | What |
|---|---|
| 8 to 12 weeks out | Pick the [format](formats/index.md), date, and name. Recruit challenge sponsors and collect their problem, data, and success criteria. |
| 6 to 8 weeks out | Write the challenge briefs and review them with sponsors. Line up tools and credits. Open registration you control. |
| 4 to 6 weeks out | Publish the challenges, rules, judging criteria, and Code of Conduct. Start marketing once the page and logos are final. |
| 1 to 2 weeks out | Send the hackathon guide. Provision accounts from the registration list. Confirm judges, mentors, and workshops. |
| Event | Run the schedule for your format. |
| Following week | Winners present on a bigger stage. Publish write-ups. |

Use the [planning checklist](planning.md) in your planning meetings.

## Format at a glance

| | [Hack day](formats/hack-day.md) | [24 hours](formats/24-hour.md) | [48 hours](formats/48-hour.md) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Length | One day | Two days, overnight | Three days |
| Example | Michigan DevFest + AI Hackathon | A2Tech360, Hack Dearborn | Hack Michigan |

In every format: teams of 1 to 5, AI and open-source technologies with sponsor tools and credits, and a submission that includes a working proof of concept, a demo, and a short presentation.
