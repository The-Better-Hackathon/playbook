# After the Event

A hackathon's value shows up after the weekend. In this model, winners get a bigger stage, partners follow up with the teams whose work they want to take further, and every winning write-up stays public so the next cohort can see what "good" looks like.

## Outcomes to plan for

- **Pilots and follow-up projects** between partners and teams
- **Hires, internships, and introductions** for the people who built the work
- **Projects that keep going:** still active at 30 and 90 days
- **A partner report** within two weeks. See [Outcomes](../partner/outcomes.md).

## Keep the momentum going

- **Publish write-ups.** A public write-up (for example on Kaggle) with the presentation and demo makes judging easier and gives every team a portfolio piece.
- **Give winners a stage.** A conference, a sponsor's all-hands, or a community event.
- **Invite teams back.** Point them to the next event, membership, and ways to keep building.
- **Share on social.** Give everyone the event hashtags.
- **Thank partners with results.** Send each partner the write-ups for their challenge, the teams they may want to meet, and an invitation to come back.
- **Check in at 30 and 90 days** with finalist and winning teams, and share what's still going.
