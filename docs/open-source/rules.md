# Set the Open Source Rules

Clear rules protect participants, maintainers, and the projects. Publish them with your official rules.

## Do this

1. **Licenses.** New projects must use an OSI-approved open source license, and teams must respect the licenses of everything they use.
2. **Credit your dependencies.** List the open source libraries, models, and datasets you used in your README.
3. **Follow each project's process:** its contribution guide, Code of Conduct, CLA or DCO requirements, and review process. Maintainers have the final say on what's merged.
4. **Be open about AI tools.** Follow each project's policy on AI-generated contributions, and disclose AI tools in your submission.
5. **No spam contributions.** One meaningful pull request beats ten trivial ones. Judges look at quality, not count.
6. **Data rules still apply:** no personal information, no data you're not allowed to use, no keys in repos.
