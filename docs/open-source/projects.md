# Recruit Projects and Maintainers

The projects make or break an open source hackathon. Choose ones with maintainers who will show up.

## Do this

1. **Pick three to eight projects** across languages and skill levels: a mix of large foundation projects, smaller community projects, and open models or datasets.
2. **Confirm a maintainer for each** who will join in person or online to answer questions and review pull requests during the event.
3. **Ask each project for starter issues** labeled "good first issue" or similar, plus a few harder ones for experienced contributors.
4. **Reach out to foundations and open source program offices** for projects, mentors, credits, and prizes. Share the [Partner track](../partner/index.md).
5. **Check each project's contribution guide,** Code of Conduct, and whether it requires a CLA (contributor license agreement) or DCO (developer certificate of origin) sign-off.
