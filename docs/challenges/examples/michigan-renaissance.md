# The Michigan Innovation Renaissance Challenge

**Hack Michigan** · Built by Michiganders. For Michigan.

*The event-wide challenge that every team can build toward, alongside the sponsor challenges.*

## 1. The problem

Michigan stands at a pivotal moment. Once the engine of American innovation, the state is ready to reclaim its position as a technology leader, not by abandoning its industrial heritage but by reimagining it for the 21st century.

Michigan ranks among the top states in manufacturing output but lags in technology adoption and digital transformation. Too often, the brightest minds leave for coastal tech hubs, and Michigan jobs are outsourced.

- Michigan graduates leave the state at higher rates than most, seeking tech opportunities elsewhere.
- Many legacy industries still run on outdated systems and manual processes.
- Small and medium-sized Michigan businesses cite technology adoption as their top barrier to growth.
- Innovators lack the infrastructure and community to build here.

!!! quote "Key insight"
    How do we use AI to modernize Michigan's cornerstone industries while keeping opportunities here, and make the state a destination, not a departure point?

## 2. Focus areas

1. **Modernizing Michigan manufacturing:** automation, predictive maintenance, quality control, supply chain.
2. **Reinvigorating Main Street:** accessible AI for small businesses and retailers.
3. **Next-gen mobility:** transportation and logistics for Michigan's automotive future.
4. **Agricultural innovation:** crop management, precision farming, food supply chains.
5. **Building Michigan's innovation ecosystem:** tools that help founders and builders stay and scale here.
6. **Sustainability and clean energy:** from factory energy use to Great Lakes water quality.
7. **Education and workforce development:** tutoring, skills gaps, retraining, career pathways.
8. **Smart urban planning and community development:** transit, infrastructure, housing, city services.

## 3. What good looks like

A working proof of concept built with AI and open-source technology that solves a real Michigan industry challenge, plus a 5 to 7 minute presentation covering the problem, the technology, the impact on Michigan businesses and communities, how it keeps opportunity here, and a roadmap to implementation.

## 4. Evaluation

| Criterion | What judges look for |
|---|---|
| Practical application | Meaningful use of AI to solve a real Michigan industry challenge |
| Michigan impact | Creates or retains opportunities for Michigan workers, businesses, and communities |
| Business value | Clear, measurable impact on productivity, efficiency, cost, or innovation |
| Implementability | A working prototype that could realistically be deployed |
| Storytelling | Positions Michigan as a technology innovator while honoring its industrial roots |

## 5. The key question

**Does your solution solve a real problem, create real value, and keep innovation rooted in Michigan?**
