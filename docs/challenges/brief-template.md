# AI Challenge Brief Template

Copy this into a doc, or fill it in with the [brief builder](brief-builder.md). Aim for one page per section at most; shorter is better.

```markdown
# [Challenge title]
**[Sponsor]** · [Event, dates, venue]

[One-line hook: the outcome, not the solution.]

## 1. The problem
[Who has the problem, why it's hard, and what it costs today. Two to four short paragraphs. No solutions.]

**Key insight:** [One or two sentences teams should remember all weekend.]

## 2. Context: why now
[What changed that makes this solvable in a weekend: data access, tool maturity, market or operational pressure.]

**Hard constraints (optional):** [Each one should rule out a kind of solution on purpose.]

## 3. What good looks like
Outcomes, not specifications. Hit these and the implementation is up to you.

1. **[Outcome]:** [Why it matters]
2. **[Outcome]:** [Why it matters]
3. **[Outcome]:** [Why it matters]

**The test:** [One question a judge can ask of any demo.]

## 4. Data and resources
[What teams have to work with: public, synthetic, or provided. Where to find it.]

**Known gaps worth going deep on:** [Where the data is thin or stale.]

## 5. Evaluation and constraints
| Criterion | What judges look for |
|---|---|
| [Impact] | [...] |
| [Innovation] | [...] |
| [Usability] | [...] |
| [Scalability] | [...] |

**Constraints:** [Non-negotiables: data rules, privacy, explainability, humans in the loop.]

## 6. Directions worth exploring
Optional. None are required, and none are the only path.
- [Stretch idea]
- [Stretch idea]

## 7. The key question
[One question that defines success.] The product is yours to invent.
```

## Checklist before you publish

- [ ] No prescribed architecture, tools, or feature list
- [ ] Outcomes describe the person with the problem, not the software
- [ ] Every constraint rules something out on purpose
- [ ] Data sources are named, and the gaps are honest
- [ ] Criteria match what the sponsor will actually judge
- [ ] The key question can be applied to any demo
- [ ] The sponsor has reviewed it
