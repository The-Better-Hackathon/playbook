# Create a Space Where People Belong

People remember whether they felt seen, welcomed, and supported. Design that on purpose, from the first website visit to the follow-up email.

## Do this

1. **Remove cost barriers.** Keep it free. Consider loaner laptops, transit or parking help, and meals for everyone.
2. **Make first-timers welcome.** Say it on the website, pair newcomers with mentors, and help people without a team find one.
3. **Reflect the community** in your speakers, mentors, judges, photos, and partners.
4. **Support local and Black-owned businesses** for food, venues, and swag where you can.
5. **Plan for everyone:** labeled food with dietary options, accessibility, a quiet room, and a prayer or reflection space.
6. **Share a Code of Conduct** and name who to contact.
