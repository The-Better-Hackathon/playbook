# Celebrate and Keep Going

AfroHacks is a starting point. The goal is that people leave with a project, a contribution, and a community to come back to.

## Do this

1. **Within 24 hours:** thank everyone, share photos and the winners, and send a short feedback survey.
2. **Celebrate publicly:** feature the winning projects and merged contributions on your channels and your partners'.
3. **Connect people to what's next:** mentors, internships, open source communities, scholarships, conferences, and the next AfroHacks.
4. **Check in at 30 and 90 days** with teams and contributors, and share what they built or merged.
5. **Send partners a report** so they come back next year.
