# Set Up the Two Paths

Every AfroHacks offers two ways to take part. Participants choose at registration and can switch on the day.

## Path 1: Build a project

1. **Choose one to three challenges.** Problems that matter to Black communities and businesses make strong challenges. Write them as [challenge briefs](../challenges/index.md) with your partners.
2. **Line up tools and credits** from technology partners, including AI models and cloud platforms.
3. **Share a starter kit** so teams can start building in the first hour. See [Starter kits](../library/starter-kits.md).
4. **Use the [How to Win workshop and team workbook](../participate/index.md)** to coach every team.

## Path 2: Contribute to open source

1. **Pick three to five open source projects** with maintainers who agree to take part, such as Linux Foundation and IBM open source projects.
2. **Get a list of starter issues** from each project before the event.
3. **Bring maintainers or mentors,** in person or online, to review and unblock contributors.
4. **Run a short setup session:** forking, branching, pull requests, and each project's contribution guide.
