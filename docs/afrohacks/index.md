**AfroHacks** began as the first hackathon at the **AfroTech Conference**, with two ways to take part:

1. **Build a project** with proprietary and open source technologies and AI.
2. **Contribute to open source** projects, such as Linux Foundation and IBM open source projects.

This section shows you how to run your own AfroHacks in your local community, at your university, or at an HBCU.

## Who it's for

| If you are… | AfroHacks helps you… |
|---|---|
| **A student organization or HBCU department** | Give students real projects, open source contributions, and connections to employers |
| **A community organization or meetup** | Bring Black builders together locally, and grow your community |
| **A professional organization chapter** | Create a hands-on event for members at every career stage |
| **A company or open source foundation** | Meet and support Black technologists, and grow your contributor community |

## What makes it AfroHacks

- **Two ways to take part:** build something new, or contribute to open source.
- **Representation at every level:** organizers, mentors, speakers, and judges who reflect the community.
- **Low barrier to entry:** free, beginner-friendly, and no team or idea required to show up.
- **A path forward:** connections to mentors, employers, open source communities, and the next event.
