# Follow Up and Celebrate

The value of a hackathon shows up after it ends. Plan these before the event starts.

## Do this

1. **Within 24 hours:** a thank-you email with photos, the winners, and a short feedback survey.
2. **Within two weeks:** celebrate the winners publicly, and send each partner their report.
3. **Give winners a bigger stage:** a conference, a tech week, or a partner's event.
4. **Connect partners with the teams** whose work they want to take further.
5. **Check in at 30 and 90 days,** and invite everyone to the next event or pop-up.
