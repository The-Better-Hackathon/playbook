# Design the Experience

People remember how the event made them feel. Plan the experience before the schedule, one stage of the journey at a time.

## Do this

1. **Walk the journey** from the first time someone sees your website to the day their project is celebrated: before, arrival, building, finishing, and after.
2. **For each stage, write one sentence** about how people should feel.
3. **Assign an owner** to every touchpoint: registration, check-in, food, space, help, submission, judging, awards, and follow-up.
4. **Plan for everyone:** accessibility, dietary needs, a quiet room, a prayer or reflection space, and a safe place to rest overnight.
5. **Brief every volunteer, mentor, partner, and sponsor.** Being welcoming is everyone's job.
