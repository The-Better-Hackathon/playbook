## Use the Skill

The **Build a Hackathon Playbook** Skill walks you through your options, reads the guidance for your model, format, and tracks, and creates a complete playbook: an overview, a timeline with real dates, team roles, a partner packet with draft challenge briefs, an event-day schedule, a participant guide, judging criteria, emails, and a follow-up plan.

1. **Get the Skill:** [download SKILL.md](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/The-Better-Hackathon/playbook/main/skills/build-hackathon-playbook/SKILL.md), or find it in the repo at [`skills/build-hackathon-playbook`](https://github.com/The-Better-Hackathon/playbook/tree/main/skills/build-hackathon-playbook).
2. **Add it to Claude:** upload it as a Skill in Claude's settings, or place it in your project's skills folder in Claude Code.
3. **Ask:** *"Help me build a playbook for our hackathon."*

No Skills where you work? The AI prompt above does the same job in any assistant.

When you share your playbook, please [credit the playbook](../library/credit.md).
