# Bring In Partners

Partners bring the real problems, the tools, and the next steps that make a hackathon matter after the weekend. Start early; most need 8 to 12 weeks.

## Do this

1. **Make a list** of the organizations your community would want to build for: companies, startups, nonprofits, universities, public agencies, and technology platforms.
2. **Send them the [Partner track](../partner/index.md).** It explains what they get, what it takes, and how to bring a challenge.
3. **Hold a short intro call** with each partner: their problem, what data they can share, and what success would look like.
4. **Write the challenge brief yourself,** with the solution taken out, and review it with the partner.
5. **Confirm tools and credits** with technology partners, and how accounts will be provisioned from your registration list.
