# Decide How People Take Part

More than one way to take part brings in more people. AfroHacks, the first hackathon at the AfroTech Conference, offered two paths: build a project, or contribute to open source.

## Do this

1. **Choose your paths.**
    - **Build a project** for a challenge or track.
    - **Contribute to open source** projects with maintainers who've agreed to take part.
    - **Bring your own project** (pop-ups).
2. **Set team size.** One to five people works well, and solo builders are welcome.
3. **Set eligibility.** Age, students or professionals, local or remote.
4. **Decide whether a project can win more than one prize,** such as a track prize and an overall prize.

5. **Choose your tracks,** such as a challenge build, open source, capture the flag, a bug bash, or a design sprint.
